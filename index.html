✨ What Is This?

microduck-lab is a fun and fascinating tool that lets you train a virtual duck (the Pollen Microduck 🦆) to become smarter using something called reinforcement learning. You don't need to know anything about programming or AI to use it — just download, run, and watch the magic happen!

Instead of using expensive special computers with fancy graphics cards (CUDA GPUs), this app works on a regular Mac computer. It's perfect for learning or just watching your duck learn to make better decisions over time.

🎮 How To Use microduck-lab

Initial Setup

When the app opens, you'll see a simple interface with a few buttons. Don't worry - it's much easier than it looks!

  1. Select a scenario: You can choose from different learning tasks for your duck, like "find food" or "avoid obstacles."
  2. Click "Start Training": This begins the AI learning process. You'll immediately see your duck start moving.
  3. Watch the progress: The right panel shows a graph - this is your duck's "score." The higher it goes, the smarter your duck gets!

Understanding What You See

Here's what each element means:

  • The Duck: The yellow duck on screen. It moves around trying to achieve goals you set.
  • Rewards: Shows how well the duck is doing. Positive numbers mean it's learning good behaviors.
  • Exploration Rate: How adventurous the duck is. At first it's high (the duck tries random actions), but it decreases as the duck learns what works.
  • Training Speed: You can adjust how fast the simulation runs - from 1x (real-time) to 10x (super fast).

📖 What Is Reinforcement Learning?

Reinforcement learning is a type of AI where a computer program learns through trial and error - just like a baby learning to walk. The duck starts with no knowledge. It tries random actions, sees what happens, and adjusts to get more "rewards." Over time, it figures out which behaviors lead to the best outcomes.

This app uses an algorithm called PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) to train the duck, which is fast and stable even on CPUs.
